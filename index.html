Every blackjack situation (your hand against each dealer upcard) is a card: 260 in all. Answer first, then rate it. Again brings it back in a minute; Hard, Good and Easy push it further out. Cards you know well come back after days, then weeks, so your time goes to the ones you don’t. New cards start with hard totals, then pairs, then soft hands. Your No surrender and Skip 10-10 settings apply here too.

Texas Hold’em. Preflop uses simplified 6-max ranges at 100 big blinds: opening when everyone before you has folded, and raising or checking in the big blind after a small-blind limp.
